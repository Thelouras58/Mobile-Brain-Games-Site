## A quick game for short breaks

Quick Tap is a fast Android game for players who enjoy reacting to on-screen prompts. Its short sessions make it easy to pick up when you have a few minutes to spare.

## Keep the challenge moving

Try to respond accurately as each round moves forward, then return for another attempt when you want a brisk puzzle-game break. For another fast-paced option, explore [Processing Speed Training]({{ '/games/processing-speed-training/' | relative_url }}).

## What to expect from a round

Quick Tap keeps the interaction direct: pay attention to the on-screen prompt and respond with a touch. The clear, short format makes it suitable for a few spare minutes without needing to work through a long tutorial. You can stop after one round or begin another when you want a quick, focused game.

## For players who like fast prompts

Quick Tap is for players who enjoy touch-based challenges and immediate feedback. It is intended for casual play, not as a test of reaction time or attention.
