## A quick game for short breaks

Quick Tap is a fast Android number-grid game. A round asks you to find and tap the numbers 1 through 25 in order. Its short sessions make it easy to pick up when you have a few minutes to spare.

## Keep the challenge moving

Scan the grid for the next number, tap it, and continue until you reach 25. Accuracy comes first; another attempt is available when you want a brisk puzzle-game break. For a different fast-paced option, explore [Processing Speed Training]({{ '/games/processing-speed-training/' | relative_url }}).

## What to expect from a round

Quick Tap keeps the interaction direct: scan the displayed numbers and touch the next one in the sequence. The clear, short format makes it suitable for a few spare minutes without needing to work through a long tutorial. You can stop after one round or begin another when you want another number-grid challenge.

## For players who like fast prompts

Quick Tap is for players who enjoy touch-based challenges and immediate feedback. It is intended for casual play, not as a test of reaction time or attention.
