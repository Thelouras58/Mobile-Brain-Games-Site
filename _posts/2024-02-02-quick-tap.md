## A quick game for short breaks

Quick Tap is a fast Android number-grid game with 30-second rounds. Find a digit that matches the displayed target and tap it before the timer runs out.

Each board contains a 4 × 6 grid of digits from 0 to 9. Stay focused on the target and aim for your best score.

## Keep the challenge moving

Each correct tap earns one point, while each incorrect tap subtracts one. After a correct tap, the board refreshes and a target is randomly selected again—so always check the target before your next move.

The grid also refreshes automatically throughout the round, keeping you scanning for a match. For a different fast-paced option, explore [Processing Speed Training]({{ '/games/processing-speed-training/' | relative_url }}).

## What to expect from a round

Check the target, scan the grid, and tap any matching digit. You only need one matching tap before the board changes; there is no number sequence to complete.

When time runs out, review your score, correct and incorrect taps, accuracy, and average response time. Your recent results and best score let you compare attempts.

## For players who like fast prompts

Quick Tap is for players who enjoy quick visual searches and balancing speed with accuracy. Its short rounds make it easy to fit a number-grid challenge into a spare moment.

Results reflect your performance within the game. They are not a clinical assessment of attention or reaction time.
