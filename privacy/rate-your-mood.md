Konstantinos Thelouras built the Daily Mood Rating app.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not use analytics, collect user data, or share user data.

## Technical stack

The app is built with Flutter and Dart and uses `cupertino_icons`, `shared_preferences`, `timezone`, and `intl`.

## Children’s privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 13.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
