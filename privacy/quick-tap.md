Konstantinos Thelouras built the Brain Game: Focus & Reaction app, presented on this site as Quick Tap.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not use analytics, collect user data, or share user data.

## Technical stack

The app is built with Flutter and Dart and uses `cupertino_icons` and `shared_preferences`.

## Children’s privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 6.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
