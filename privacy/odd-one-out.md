Konstantinos Thelouras built the **Odd One Out** app.

## Data collection and privacy

This application is designed with privacy as a priority.

- **No analytics:** The app does not use third-party analytics or tracking software.
- **No data collection:** No personal information or gameplay data is collected or transmitted to external servers.
- **Local storage:** Best scores and game history are stored only on your device.
- **No data sharing:** The app does not share user data with third parties.

## Technical stack

The app is built with Flutter and Dart and uses `cupertino_icons`, `shared_preferences` for local high scores and accessibility settings, and `provider` for state management.
