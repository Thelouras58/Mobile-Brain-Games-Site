**Effective date: March 2, 2023**

Konstantinos Thelouras built Pattern Lock: Brain Games as an ad-supported app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## Information collection and use

For a better experience, the app may require certain personally identifiable information. Information requested by the app is retained on your device and is not collected by the developer directly.

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Google Analytics data is used only for analytics and monitoring the health of the app.

When an error occurs, third-party products may collect log data, including IP address, device name, operating-system version, app configuration, the time and date of use, and other statistics.

## Crash reporting and error monitoring

To maintain and improve the stability and performance of Pattern Lock: Brain Games, the app uses Sentry for crash reporting and error monitoring. When a crash or error occurs, Sentry may receive error messages and stack traces, app state and usage immediately before the error, the error date and time, device and app details, device status such as battery level and connection type, an identifier used to group errors from the same device, and an IP address if collected.

The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or user-generated content in crash reports. Any accidental transmission of personal information is subject to data scrubbing and is not used for another purpose. Crash and error data is used only to diagnose and fix technical issues, monitor app performance, and improve the user experience; it is not used for marketing, profiling, or tracking individual users.

Sentry processes this data as a data processor and may store it in the European Union or the United States, depending on configuration. See [Sentry’s Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) for more information.

## Security

The developer uses commercially acceptable means to protect personal information, but no internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure or reliable.

## Children’s privacy
