**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

Konstantinos Thelouras, operating as Mobile Brain Games, provides **Memory Match: Brain Games**, also known as Simple Memory Game. This policy covers the Android app, including its free, ad-supported features and optional one-time Pro purchase.

## Information collection and use

The app has no account registration. We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment-card details through the app. If you email us for support or to exercise your privacy rights, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include so that we can respond.

The app uses **Google Play Billing**, **RevenueCat**, **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob/UMP)**, and **Firebase Analytics** for purchases, advertising, consent management, analytics, and diagnostics as described below. These services can process identifiers and technical data even though you do not create an app account. We do not sell personal data.

## One-time Pro purchase and restoration

Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; the developer does not receive your card or bank details.

RevenueCat processes your purchase status, Google purchase token and transaction data, an anonymous app-user identifier, and necessary technical information, such as app and SDK versions and device operating system. We use this information to validate purchases, unlock Pro, restore your entitlement, and understand purchase activity. The anonymous identifier distinguishes an app user without requiring a name or email address; it does not mean that purchase records are completely anonymous.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Free users may receive ads from Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners can process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses and information derived from them (such as approximate location), app and ad interactions, and ad impression-revenue data. They use this information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and reporting. AdMob can also process technical and diagnostic information about the app, device, and ad performance.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising consent and privacy choices. You can review or change your advertising consent in the app through **Settings → Privacy choices**. Ad delivery and personalization depend on your choices and applicable requirements.

Users who buy Pro no longer receive ads. Purchase validation and the analytics and diagnostics described in this policy can still apply to Pro users.

## Analytics and diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand how people use the app, measure feature engagement and sessions, and improve the experience and reliability. The data can include app usage and interaction events, device information and identifiers, operating-system and app versions, session information, and performance data. Analytics can also include purchase events, ad impressions and associated revenue, and approximate location derived from IP information.

Technical and diagnostic data help us investigate errors and performance issues. These can include app configuration, timestamps, and information about how the app and its SDKs perform. Collection is used for usage analysis, measurement, reporting, and product improvement, as well as diagnostics.

## Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including support, purchase validation and restoration, and applicable legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of data, the service involved, its configured retention settings, and any legal requirements. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any related follow-up.

RevenueCat customer data can be deleted on request. Google may need to retain purchase records for legal, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention obligations; those records are governed by Google's own obligations and policies. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete records held by these providers.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including limiting access to data used to operate and support the app. Google and RevenueCat use encryption in transit for data sent to their services. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is completely secure.

## International processing

Google, RevenueCat, and their service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Privacy laws may differ between countries. Where required, international transfers are subject to safeguards provided under applicable data-protection law; the providers' privacy information below explains their arrangements.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

For access or deletion requests, including deletion of RevenueCat customer data, email [hello@mobilebraingames.com](mailto:hello@mobilebraingames.com). Mention Memory Match and describe your request. We may need limited information to verify the request and locate records associated with your app installation or purchase. Do not send payment-card or bank details. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and explain any records that must be retained or requests that must be directed to Google. Advertising consent can also be changed through **Settings → Privacy choices**.
