**Effective date: August 28, 2026**

Neuroscience Quiz - Trivia is developed by Konstantinos Thelouras ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Brain Trivia mobile application (the "App").

## Information we collect

Brain Trivia does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other contact details.

The app uses the following services to help understand how it performs and to fix problems:

- **Firebase Analytics** may collect usage information, such as app interactions, device and app identifiers, device model, operating-system version, and general diagnostic information.
- **Sentry** may collect crash reports and performance information, including technical details such as device and app version, operating-system version, IP address, and information related to an error.

The app does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information.

## How we use information

Information is used only to understand how the app is used, monitor and improve reliability and performance, and diagnose and fix crashes or other technical issues. The developer does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Third-party services

Firebase Analytics and Sentry process information under their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Data Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)

## Data retention and security

Information collected by service providers is retained only for as long as needed for analytics, error monitoring, and the purposes described here, subject to retention settings and legal requirements. Reasonable steps are taken to protect information, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

## Children’s privacy

Brain Trivia is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer so appropriate action can be taken.
