Konstantinos Thelouras built the Quick Tap: Attention Trainer app, presented on this site as Processing Speed Training.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not use analytics, collect user data, or share user data. Only the default information handled by Google Play is used.

## Technical stack

The app is built with Flutter, Dart, and Flame.

## Children’s privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 6.
