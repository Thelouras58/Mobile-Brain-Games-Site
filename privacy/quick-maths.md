## Data collection and sharing

The app does not use analytics, collect user data, or share user data.

## Technical stack

The app is built with Flutter and Dart and uses `cupertino_icons`, `shared_preferences`, and `provider`.

## Children’s privacy

This service does not address anyone under the age of 3.
