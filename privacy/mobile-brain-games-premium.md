**Last updated: June 26, 2026**

Konstantinos Thelouras (also referred to as “we”, “us”, or “our”) built the **Mobile Brain Games Premium** app (the “Service”) as a commercial, freemium application for Android and iOS. This page explains the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use the Service, and the terms that govern its use.

## Privacy policy

### 1. Information we collect

| Source | What is collected | Where it is stored | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Local device storage** (SharedPreferences) | User profile data (`userId`, `joinDate`, `premiumStatus`, streaks, total games, play time, per-game stats, high scores, and best reaction times); selected locale; demo-game-play tracking flags | On the user’s device only | Persist app state, enable offline play, remember language preference |
| **Firebase Anonymous Authentication** | Anonymous UID generated by Firebase Auth | Firebase Auth (cloud) | Provide a stable identifier for syncing the local profile across devices |
| **Cloud Firestore** (via `SyncService`) | Full user profile and a history of game results (`gameId`, `category`, `score`, `duration`, `metadata`) | `users/{uid}` collection and `users/{uid}/history` sub-collection | Back up user progress, enable cross-device sync, and restore data after reinstallation |
| **Firebase Analytics** | Events such as `app_open`, `screen_view`, `game_start`, `game_complete`, `paywall_view`, `purchase_attempt`, `purchase_success`, `purchase_failure`, and `ui_interaction`; user properties including `is_premium`, `total_games_played`, `current_streak`, and `best_streak` | Google’s analytics servers | Understand use of the Service, monitor app health, improve the experience, and measure conversion to the pro version |
| **RevenueCat** | Transaction identifier, receipt data, anonymous device identifier, and purchase status for the Mobile Brain Games Premium Pro entitlement | RevenueCat servers | Verify a one-time purchase and unlock pro features |
| **Error and crash data** (only when a crash occurs) | Stack traces, error messages, device model, OS version, app version, battery level, network type, locale, timestamp, and a synthetic device identifier | Sent to Firebase if Crashlytics is enabled in the future | Diagnose and fix technical issues |

#### What we do not collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, or location. No advertising SDKs are included, and the app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or health data.

### 2. How we use information

- Store game progress, sync it to the cloud, and restore it on new devices.
- Verify one-time purchase status through RevenueCat to enable pro features.
- Use aggregate analytics and performance information to guide feature development.
- Use optional crash logs to resolve bugs.

### 3. Third-party services

| Service | What it may collect | Privacy policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Firebase** (Google Analytics, Auth, Firestore) | Event data, anonymous UID, and stored documents | [Firebase Data Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) |
| **RevenueCat** | Purchase receipt, device identifier, and entitlement status | [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) |
| **Google Play Billing** | Transaction IDs and payment method, handled by Google | [Google Play Privacy](https://play.google.com/about/privacy/) |
| **Apple App Store** | Transaction IDs and receipt data, handled by Apple | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/) |

We do not share data with other parties except as required to process payments or operate these services.

### 4. One-time purchase and pro unlock

The Service uses a freemium model. Five games are free, while nine additional games become permanently available after a single lifetime purchase of Mobile Brain Games Premium Pro. RevenueCat manages this one-time entitlement: it records the transaction identifier and receipt, validates the purchase with the applicable store, and grants the entitlement. There is no recurring billing, auto-renewal, or future charge.

### 5. Permissions

| Platform | Permission | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | `android.permission.INTERNET` | Network communication for Firebase, RevenueCat, and sync |
| Android | `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Detect connectivity for online features |

| iOS | No special permissions declared | The app does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts |

### 6. Security

Data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS. Firebase and RevenueCat provide their own data-at-rest security measures. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

### 7. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group. Contact us if you become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data.

### 8. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy from time to time. A revised version will be posted in the app and this page will show an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
