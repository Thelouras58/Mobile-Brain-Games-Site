**Start with the activity the player already enjoys.** A matching game asks players to find picture pairs; Quick Maths is a timed arithmetic game; Pattern Lock asks players to repeat a pattern. The full collection offers several types of short games. These are play options, not assessments or promises of educational or medical benefits. Check each product page for its verified platforms and store facts before installing.

## Which game is the easiest place to begin?

[Simple Memory Game]({{ '/games/simple-memory-game/' | relative_url }}) uses a familiar card-matching format on Android and iOS. You can [try the browser version]({{ '/memory-match/' | relative_url }}) together before installing it.

## What if we prefer numbers?

[Quick Maths]({{ '/games/quick-maths/' | relative_url }}) is for players ready for mental arithmetic under a timer. A round has ten questions and gives ten seconds for each answer, with Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels. It is an ad-free game, but it is not an untimed counting activity.

## What if we prefer visual patterns?

[Pattern Lock: Brain Games]({{ '/games/pattern-lock-brain-games/' | relative_url }}) offers pattern recall and number drawing, plus the Hack The Pattern variation. It is an offline Android game. Review the game's instructions when deciding whether it suits a particular player.

## What is in the full collection?

[Mobile Brain Games Premium]({{ '/games/mobile-brain-games-premium/' | relative_url }}) combines short memory, focus, speed, rule-switching, and word games. It is free to install, with optional upgrades that unlock more games, unlimited daily play, and deeper progress views. The current Google Play listing rates it for everyone; families should still decide whether its pace and wording suit the individual player.

## How should we choose?

Choose by the activity, pace, store information, and age rating that fit the player. Memory Match offers a familiar starting point, Quick Maths suits players comfortable with timed arithmetic, and Pattern Lock or the full collection provide broader challenges. For more activity ideas, see [Card-Matching Games for Kids and Families]({{ '/blog/card-matching-games-for-kids/' | relative_url }}) and the [Quick Maths guide]({{ '/blog/quick-maths-games-for-kids/' | relative_url }}).
