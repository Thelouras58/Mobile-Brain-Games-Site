## How card matching works

Turn over a card, remember its position, and look for its matching pair. Card-matching games are easy to explain and work well as short activities for kids, families, or anyone who enjoys a relaxed puzzle.

## Make each round your own

Players can take their time learning the board or try to finish a round faster. The goal is simply to find the pairs and enjoy the next challenge.
