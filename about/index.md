Mobile Brain Games publishes Android puzzle, memory, word, maths, reaction, and casual games. The Google Play listings identify the developer as **Konstantinos Panagiotis Thelouras**. This website presents the publisher's own games and explains their play styles.

## How this site describes the games

Each [game page]({{ '/games/' | relative_url }}) links to its Google Play listing. Store names, availability, ads, and purchase information are checked against those listings; the date appears on each game page. A game's own description is the source for feature details. If the listing changes, the live store page is the current source for installation and purchase choices.

The [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) offers practical play ideas from the publisher's perspective. Articles link to background sources where relevant. Games are for entertainment and are not medical, diagnostic, or therapeutic tools.

## Contact and support

For questions about a game or this website, email [hello@mobilebraingames.com](mailto:hello@mobilebraingames.com). For app-specific privacy information, open the privacy policy linked from that app's Google Play listing.
