Flame privacy and analytics

Flame stores game progress and your analytics choice locally on your device or browser. Analytics is optional: the full game works when you decline.

If you accept, Google Firebase Analytics collects app or browser identifiers, device and app information, sessions, engagement, and gameplay interactions: tutorials, run starts and ends, scores, character unlocks, power-ups and virtual shop activity. Virtual coins are not real-money revenue. Google may derive approximate location from network information.

We use this information to understand how the game is used and improve it. We do not set a custom user ID, enable Google Signals, or use this integration for advertising or remarketing. We do not request advertising tracking permission or collect IDFA through this analytics build.

Change your choice through Privacy / Analytics on the game's home screen. Declining stops future collection; it does not delete information already sent. Events before acceptance are discarded. Clearing local storage removes your saved choice and may also remove game progress.

Web analytics applies to mobilebraingames.com/flame/. Google processes collected information under its privacy policy. For publisher contact and privacy requests, email hello@mobilebraingames.com.
