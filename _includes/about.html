About Mobile Brain Games

Small games, clear challenges, and no complicated setup.

Mobile Brain Games is developed and published by Konstantinos Panagiotis Thelouras. The collection includes short puzzle, memory, word, maths, and reaction games for Android, with selected iOS and browser experiences.

Each game page explains how to play and links to available Android, iOS, or browser versions. Availability and features can vary by platform.

The games are made for entertainment. Descriptions of attention, memory, or other skills refer to the activity used during play and are not medical, diagnostic, educational, or therapeutic claims.
